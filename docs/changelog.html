[0.6.0] — 2026-11-20

A film passed romp check --strict on 0.5.0 with faults a viewer saw at once: an app screenshot drawn too small to read, a vehicle and papers crossing a title, a phone mock 2% off the frame's edge, and a speech bubble that appeared below its ground line and snapped up to it. Why each got through, and what catches it now:

  • No rule knew what a screenshot was, or how big its text came out.
  • The props were untracked, or drawn inside one SVG: the check only follows [data-romp] elements, and --strict only counts untagged *tracked* objects. The tracked ones crossed the title in under the 0.2 s overlap limit.
  • cropped allowed any object 25% outside the frame.
  • A 60 px move in one frame is under the strobe limit (~120 px at 1080 wide), and the spike rule needs motion on both sides, so a snap at a scene start passed.

Added

  • legibility: a new screen role for a screenshot or screen recording, with its content's native width (390 for a phone, 1440 for a desktop page) and an optional minTextPx (16). Flags a screen whose body text renders under legiblePx (20 px on a 1080 px short side, in proportion) for more than legibleSeconds (0.5 s). Zooming is fine; a zoomed or cropped screen passes. trackScreen(name, nativeWidth) in romp/remotion, data-romp-native-width and data-romp-min-text-px in the DOM.
  • jump: a move over ~40 px (scaled from 886 px wide; jumpPx) in one frame between still frames, two either side. Not at a scene cut, not when the object changes size that frame.
  • anchor: a text with a ground (a y in frame px) that shows below it, or with an anchor (its speaker's tracked name) that drifts more than anchorPx (10% of the short side) from it. data-romp-anchor, data-romp-ground; boxGap().
  • romp check --strict warnings about untracked <svg>, <canvas>, <img> and <video> drawn over a tracked text, which the check can't see into. untrackedOver(root) lists them; Report.warnings carries them.
  • A sample's allowCrop can be a share (allowCrop: 0.1, data-romp-allow-crop="0.1").
  • Demo: "Plant a snap" makes the caption type in low and jump up.

Changed

  • cropped: a screen or focus that would fit in the frame may have 1% outside it (screenCroppedFraction), not 25%. One bigger than the frame is a zoom and isn't checked.
  • overlap: a prop drawn over a text is flagged after two frames in a row (propOverlapSeconds, 0.04 s), not 0.2 s. Two texts still get 0.2 s.
  • Speed checks (strobe, teleport, jump) skip frames at a declared scene cut.
  • Docs: what the check can't see (untagged elements, props inside one SVG or canvas) and how to track them.

[0.5.0] — 2026-11-20

Words on screen and the soundtrack get checked too. A frame-by-frame review of a real portrait film turned up faults the motion check couldn't see: captions cut off mid-word when their scene ended, lines whole for barely a second, a speech bubble covering the very button it described, bubbles running off the edge, papers and dots crossing a title, empty bubbles, titles that sat blank under their underline while they retyped after every cut, and the music dropping out for half a second at segment joins. Each of those is now a rule, and the timing helpers make the right lengths the default.

Added

  • Text rules in the motion check, for objects tracked with a role ("text", "focus" or "prop"): offsafe (a legible text outside the safe area; safeArea() defaults to 5% of the long side at its ends and 3.5% of the short side, so 38/96 px at 1080×1920), covers (a text over the focus box), overlap (two texts, or a prop drawn over a text; a prop drawn under it is a backdrop), unfinished (gone, or its scene cut, before every character shows), unread (whole for less than its reading time), empty (a box with no words, or a heading still typing after it appears) and stale (opt-in: nothing moves long after the words were read). Each has its thresholds as options and an allow list (names, or a prefix*). New Sample fields: role, chars: { shown, total }, text, words, heading, z, allow; new options scenes (cuts), strict, safe, reading and the thresholds.
  • romp/timing: readingTime(text, { wpm = 180, min = 1.5 }), wordCount, captionTiming(text, { cps = 30 }) (typeFrames, holdFrames, totalFrames, typed(frame)), timeline(scenes) (scene lengths from their captions, transitions that never land on a caption's reading window, 1.2 s act cards, and a check with the covered spans and cuts to spread into a check entry) and captionAt. Also exported from romp and romp/remotion.
  • romp/remotion: trackText(name, text, { typed, heading }), track(name, { role, allow, z }) and sceneSequence(scene). A CheckEntry takes any analyze() option.
  • collectDom reads data-romp-role, data-romp-text, data-romp-chars-shown, data-romp-chars-total, data-romp-words, data-romp-heading, data-romp-allow and data-romp-z, and records each element's paint order.
  • Audio check: dropouts(buffer) in romp/audio flags 0.1–1 s of near silence (30 dB under the bed) with sound on both sides; readWav, rmsWindows, formatDropouts. romp check --audio file.wav runs it, alone or with an entry. crossfadeBed(synth, bus, sections, { fade }) writes one continuous bed with equal-power crossfades at every join.
  • romp check --strict: untagged tracked objects count as props for overlap, and stale runs. formatReport widens its kind column for the new kinds.
  • Demo: a caption typed in with captionTiming and tracked as text, and a "Plant a text bug" toggle that cuts it off mid-word.

Changed

  • The text rules are on by default for anything with a role. A film that tags nothing reports exactly what it did before.

[0.4.2] — 2026-11-19

Fixed

  • The lost rule's camera test, done properly: the three.js collector splits each object's on-screen move into the camera's part (the same world point through last frame's camera and this one) and its own; leaving the frame mostly by the camera's part means the camera moved on (Sample.cameraLeft). 0.4.1's test (where it was last in view, through today's camera) missed slow cameras. Tested on three camera-driven films: from 40–56 flags per film to the few real exits.

[0.4.1] — 2026-11-19

Fixed

  • The motion check's lost rule no longer flags objects a moving camera leaves behind, first try: the three.js collector recorded each object's world position, and an object that stayed put in its world while it left the frame was spared. Superseded by 0.4.2 (objects that move a little, like a bobbing pin, slipped through).

[0.4.0] — 2026-11-18

Added

  • romp/particles: a deterministic particle emitter. Each particle's launch comes from a seeded hash of its index and its flight is closed-form (gravity, wind, linear drag, sway, spin, confetti flip), so particlesAt(frame) is a pure function of the frame: any frame, any order, in a live viewer, Remotion or the motion check. Particles bounce and come to rest on a ground (a y, or the polyline you gave stage.ground()), grow in and fade out by default (not pops), and come out as plain data. Presets confetti, sparks, snow and fountain; particleStyle (DOM), drawParticles (canvas), and particleSample to check a burst as one object.
  • trackBurst(name, particles) in romp/remotion: tracks a particle burst as one object for romp check.
  • Sound as a signal: listen(schedule) in romp/audio runs a schedule silently and records every voice it plays; the Score answers envelopeAt(t, { bus?, voice?, decay? }), beatAt(t) and onsetsBetween(t0, t1) as pure functions of film time, for visuals that react to the soundtrack without a live analyser.
  • romp/theatre: seekSequence, followSequence, objectsRow and theatreReport, so a Theatre.js sequence can host romp-baked motion and the motion check can step it.
  • romp/motion-canvas: playBaked (baked poses inside a scene, by its time), centred, and motionCanvasReport / byKey / nodeSample to run the motion check over a Motion Canvas project.
  • romp/lottie and romp check --lottie anim.json: the motion check for lottie-web animations (After Effects exports), every layer through its parents and precomps: a layer cut in at full size is a pop.
  • @theatre/core, @motion-canvas/core, @motion-canvas/2d and lottie-web are optional peer dependencies; none of the adapters import them.
  • Demo: sparks where the flung character hits, tracked as one burst (the check stays clean), and a shelf that brightens on every kick drum, read from the score.

[0.3.1] — 2026-11-18

Fixed

  • Sideways hits are impacts: a body whose horizontal speed reverses sharply (a wall, or a neighbour side-on) records an impact with side: true, so the motion check no longer reads a bounce off a wall as a kink. landed ignores side hits, and impactResponse squashes half as much for them.

[0.3.0] — 2026-11-18

Added

  • The motion check's lost rule: a character that has been in frame leaves it entirely and stays out for more than lostSeconds (1 s) is flagged; mark a real exit with allowExit (option, track(name, { allowExit }), userData.allowExit).
  • impactResponse(body, frame, seed, speedRef, { squash, widen }): tune how far a landing squashes and how much of it spreads sideways, so characters piled against each other don't overlap on screen.

Fixed

  • Demo: the flung character no longer bounces out of the picture (walls above the frame), can't tunnel through others (bullet), turns about its centre instead of its feet, and every character is drawn the same round shape as its body, so what you see is what collides. The planted-bug sticker keeps dark text in dark mode.

[0.2.0] — 2026-11-18

Added

  • Motion check: cropped. A tracked object that's shown and on screen but has more than croppedFraction (default 0.25) of its own box outside the frame for more than croppedSeconds (default 0.5 s) in a row. Runs where it's entering or leaving (its on-frame share only rises or only falls) and covered frames are exempt; opt out per object with track(name, { allowCrop: true }) / data-romp-allow-crop, userData.allowCrop in three.js, or allowCrop names. collectDom and motionReport now report each object's box; inFrameShare() and screenBox() are exported.
  • poseAt / poseAtTime take { discrete: "nearest" | "previous" } for how booleans and other non-numeric fields blend between baked frames.
  • Stage.add(actor) sets actor.stage before build(); Stage.pose(t) hands every actor with an apply(pose, t) its interpolated pose, NaN poses as { shown: false }.
  • Drop: foot (a contact circle), spinAt and tilt that unwind to zero at touchdown, z: [from, to] depth drift arrested on contact, rock direction, ledge with a frame offset; flight(frame) and framePose(frame). Body gains gravityScale; Leap gains gravity and frame.
  • A docs site at https://isaacrowntree.com/romp/: a live demo (a springy wordmark knocked over, a pile of hatted characters whose hats follow through with jiggleParts, play/pause/scrub with sound on the audio clock, the same soundtrack rendered offline to a WAV, and the motion check with a "plant a bug" toggle), guides, the API reference (checked against the exports at build time) and this changelog. The demo's source is examples/demo/.

Changed

  • romp check and motionReport now pass the frame's height, so the cropped check runs by default (this is why it's 0.2.0): an object parked half off the edge that passed before is now reported.

[0.1.1] — 2026-11-18

Added

  • jiggleParts(parts, dt, strength?, lag?): secondary motion for several parts at once, each lagging the one before.
  • romp check --arg <json>: the entry may export a function of the argument, so one entry covers several cuts (languages, variants).

Fixed

  • bin/romp.mjs is executable, so bunx romp / npx romp work after installing from GitHub.

[0.1.0] — 2026-11-18

Added

  • romp/core: a planck.js (Box2D) Stage that bakes a scene once at a fixed 240 Hz and stores one pose per frame, in screen space (y down) or y-up; the actor protocol (build / control / sample); preset actors Body (with recorded impacts), SpringyBody (welds where it lands on its ground), Drop and Leap; collision layers; poseAt (frames) and poseAtTime (seconds).
  • Character motion as pure functions: impactResponse (squash, blink, follow-through and jiggle from real impacts), landing, hop, idle, blinkAt, boing, jiggle, rock, nearImpact; curves ease, spring, ringOut, stagger.
  • Clock: real-time playback driving seek(t) on requestAnimationFrame, following the audio clock while sound plays.
  • romp/check: the motion check (analyze: pops, strobes, teleports, spikes, with whips, covered frames, impacts and seamless swaps exempt), formatReport, and collectDom for data-romp elements.
  • romp/remotion (track) and romp/three (motionReport).
  • romp/audio: synthesised voices and instruments, createMix, and one schedule played live (playLive) or rendered offline (renderOffline, renderWav); romp/audio/cartoon voices.
  • CLI: romp check (Remotion compositions in headless Chromium) and romp sound (render a schedule to a loudness-normalised WAV).
  • Live demo page in docs/ and a generated hero GIF.
